# Source notes

Primary materials used to design and interpret the experiment:

1. Jacob Biamonte et al., ["Quantum Machine Learning," *Nature* 549,
   195-202 (2017)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature23474).
2. [First Workshop on Quantum Tensor Networks in Machine Learning, NeurIPS
   2020 - accepted papers](https://tensorworkshop.github.io/NeurIPS2020/accepted_papers.html).
3. Xiao-Yang Liu and Yiming Fang, ["Quantum Tensor Networks for Variational
   Reinforcement Learning" (NeurIPS 2020 workshop)](https://tensorworkshop.github.io/NeurIPS2020/accepted_papers/NIPS_2020_Workshop_Yiming%20%281%29.pdf).
4. [Second Workshop on Quantum Tensor Networks in Machine Learning, NeurIPS
   2021 - accepted papers](https://tensorworkshop.github.io/NeurIPS2021/accepted_papers.html).
5. Keyi Wang et al., ["FinRL Contests: Benchmarking Data-driven Financial
   Reinforcement Learning Agents"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02281).
6. [FinRL Contest 2025 overview](https://finrl-contest.readthedocs.io/en/latest/finrl2025/overview.html).
7. [FinRL Contest 2025 stock-trading task](https://finrl-contest.readthedocs.io/en/latest/finrl2025/task1.html).
8. [Official FinRL Contest 2025 starter repository](https://github.com/Open-Finance-Lab/FinRL_Contest_2025).
9. [AI4Finance Foundation FinRL repository](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL).
10. [Processed FinRL Contest dataset used in this
    run](https://huggingface.co/datasets/benstaf/nasdaq_2013_2023).

The report does not rely on unverified citations or performance claims from the
original AP Research paper. The local paper was used only to recover the
earlier project's stated design: 15 stocks, 13 features, multiple horizons,
and the ANN/QINN comparison.
